01

THE CHALLENGE

If the data is wrong, even the best process cannot work reliably.

Duplicate or incomplete data, faulty transfers and poorly connected systems create rework, delays and avoidable errors.

These issues often become visible only during a system change. That is why it pays to review data quality and data flows early—before problems are carried into a new system.

02

MY VIEW

A new system does not make bad data better.

Many data problems exist long before a migration begins. If they are not identified and cleaned up first, they simply move into the new system.
